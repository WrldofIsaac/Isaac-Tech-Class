APPLE TRUTH VANDALISM

I used google's browser developer tools (inspect element) to temporarily "vandalize" a website. What I decided to add was green text (previously charcoal color) symbolizing talking about losing money.In which comes the second part, which shows the red text that has been enlarged to 52px, and changed the Blue BUY button to a red Don't But button to prevent users from not buying the product. Description is also red to show how heinous this phone deal is. But also intentionally changed the word For to From to show audience not to take this product serious.
